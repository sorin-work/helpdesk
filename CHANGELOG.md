# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-07

First release.

- Customer portal: sign-up, requests with attachments, replies and marking a
  request as solved
- Agent workspace: queues for mine, unassigned, all open, overdue and solved,
  with filters, sorting and live counts
- Statuses with allowed transitions, priorities and SLA targets per priority in
  business hours or 24/7, paused while pending or on hold
- Business hours with a time zone, per-weekday windows and holidays
- Round-robin assignment with agent availability
- Internal notes, canned responses with placeholders, tags and full-text search
- Reports: volume, first-response and resolution times, SLA compliance by
  priority and per agent
- Admin pages for people, business hours, SLA targets and assignment
- Docker Compose setup with healthchecks and optional demo data
